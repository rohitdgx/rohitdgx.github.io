Questions

Where is the site hosted?

On a free, fast host (GitHub Pages or Netlify). There's no monthly hosting fee. If you own a domain, we show you how to connect it.

Do I need to write the text?

No. Send your links and a few lines about what you do. We write the headline, services and project blurbs, and you approve them.

Can I edit it later?

Yes. You get the files. Small changes are easy to make yourself, or take the $9 update plan and send us changes by email.

How is the rate calculated?

(Income goal + yearly costs) ÷ (1 − tax %) gives the revenue you need. We divide that by billable hours: working weeks × hours per week × billable share. Admin, sales and learning time aren't billable, which is why the share is usually 50-70%.
