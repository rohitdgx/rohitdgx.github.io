What this checks, in plain words

MX: who receives your mail

Shows which company runs your inbox (Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Zoho and others). The fixes below are written for that provider.

SPF: who may send as you

A list of services allowed to send email for your domain. Missing or broken SPF is one of the most common reasons business mail lands in spam.

DKIM: a signature on every email

Your provider signs each message so receivers can tell nobody changed it. Gmail and Yahoo expect it from businesses.

DMARC: what to do with fakes

Tells Gmail and Outlook what to do with mail that pretends to be you. Since November 2025 Gmail rejects bulk mail from domains without it.

Does a full score guarantee the inbox?

No. These records prove you are who you say you are. What you send, how often, and whether people open it still count.
