Every concept is ready to shoot or design

Each concept is a different angle, so Meta treats it as new. Here's one, written for a made-up skincare brand.

Concept 4 of 10 · Angle: the skeptic who almost returned it · example for a fictional brand
Hooks (3)
1. "I almost returned it. Then day 6 happened."
2. "My dermatologist asked what I changed."
3. "I've wasted $400 on serums. This one is $28."
Primary text
I nearly sent it back after 5 days. On day 6 my chin breakout was flat. Three ingredients, no fragrance, and a 30-day refund if it doesn't work for you.
Headline
Clear skin in 14 days, or your money back
UGC script (30s)
0-3s: hook 1 to camera, holding the jar. 3-12s: show the breakout photo from day 1. 12-22s: apply at night, say the 3 ingredients. 22-30s: day 14 close-up, "30-day refund, link below."
Static ad
Image text: "I almost returned it." Sub-line: "Day 14 →". Show before/after only if your category allows it.

Questions

Do you make the videos and images too?

No. We write the concepts, scripts and image text. Your creators, editor or designer produce them, or you shoot them on a phone. That's why the price stays low.

Will the ads follow Meta's ad rules?

We write to Meta's advertising standards: no claims about a person's health, body or finances, and no "Are you…?" lines that point at personal traits. You still review before publishing, and some categories (health, finance) have extra rules.

How do monthly plans work?

Send your product details once. Every week we send 10 new concepts. Reply with your winners and losers, and the next batch builds on what worked.

Can my agency resell these?

Yes. Agency plans are white-label: no DGX name anywhere. Use them for your clients however you like.
