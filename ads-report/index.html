Is my client data uploaded anywhere?

No. The file is read by your own browser. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else. You can turn off your internet after the page loads and it still works.

Which export works best?

Export at the Ad level with these columns: Amount spent, Results, Cost per result, Impressions, Reach, Frequency, Link clicks, CTR (link), Purchases, Purchases conversion value and Purchase ROAS. Campaign or ad set level exports also work.

What do the warnings mean?

Frequency over 3 means the same people keep seeing the ad, so results usually get more expensive. Link CTR under 0.8% usually points to a weak hook or image. Ads that spent more than twice the average cost per result without a result are pause candidates.

Does it work for Google or TikTok?

It's built for Meta exports today. Other exports with "Amount spent" or "Cost" and "Results" or "Conversions" columns may partly work.
