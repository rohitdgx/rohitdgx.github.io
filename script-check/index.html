What to fix

  • Your fixes will show here.

Hook

The time before your first promise, question or stakes. Under 15 seconds keeps more people past the intro.

Pacing

Sections over 90 seconds with no question, twist or "but" get flagged. Those are common drop spots.

Sentences

Lines over 25 words are hard to say on camera and hard to follow. Split them.

Fillers

"Basically", "actually", "just", "really" and friends add seconds without adding meaning.
