Questions

We built a writing process that does the research and first draft fast, then we check every script for hook, pacing and facts. Less time per script means a lower price for you.

Send 1–2 of your best videos with your order. We match the pacing, sentence length and phrases you use. Every script gets free revisions.

No one honestly can. We guarantee the script: a hook inside 15 seconds, no dead sections, checked facts, and a delivery time of 24 hours.

No. It works for anyone who records from a script: talking-head explainers, podcasts with a narrative intro, and course videos.
