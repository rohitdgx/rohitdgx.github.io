06 — FAQ

Before you DM us.

Who do you work with?

Everyone who posts video: content creators, YouTubers, founders, business owners, coaches, SaaS and ecom brands, real estate, and agencies that need extra editing hands under their own name.

What do I need to send?

Raw footage. A phone recording works. If you don't know what to say, we give you hooks and topics before you record.

Which platforms do you edit for?

Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, X and LinkedIn, plus long-form YouTube and ads for Meta and TikTok.

Do you work with agencies?

Yes. We work white-label as your overflow team, in your style and under your brand, so you can take on more clients.

How do I reach you?

How much does it cost?
